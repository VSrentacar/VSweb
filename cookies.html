Cookie Policy

Effective: 13 August 2026 · Very Special Rent a Car

This policy describes storage on your device when you use our website, which is the property of Very Special Rent a Car.

1. Essential storage

We store your language choice (EN / MK / AL) in the browser’s localStorage under the key “vs-lang”. This is strictly necessary to remember your preference and does not require consent under ePrivacy rules for essential storage. You can clear it in your browser settings.

2. Google Maps

The locations section loads a Google Map (iframe, and optionally the Maps JavaScript API if an API key is configured). Google may set cookies or collect technical data according to Google’s privacy policy. Loading the map is part of providing the locations feature you request by visiting that section.

3. Analytics and marketing

We do not currently set advertising or analytics cookies. If we add them later, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent before non-essential cookies run.

4. Manage cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking Google cookies may limit the map. Essential language storage can be removed by clearing site data.
