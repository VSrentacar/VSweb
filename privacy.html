This Privacy Policy explains how Very Special Rent a Car (“we”, “us”, “our”) processes personal data. This website, brand and all associated content are the exclusive property of Very Special Rent a Car.

1. Data controller

The controller is Very Special Rent a Car, Skopje, North Macedonia. Contact: info.vsrentacar@gmail.com · +389 75 622 558.

2. Data we collect

  • Identity and contact data: full name, phone number, email address.
  • Rental data: pickup and return locations, dates and times, requested vehicle, optional notes (for example flight number or child seat).

We process data to respond to booking requests, conclude and perform the rental contract, provide customer support, prevent fraud and vehicle misuse, meet accounting, tax and traffic-law duties, and defend legal claims. 4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with insurers, payment processors, IT and hosting providers, and public authorities when required by law, and only as necessary. Google Maps may process technical data if you load the map (see Cookie Policy).

5. Retention

Enquiry data is kept as long as needed to handle the request and follow-up, then deleted or anonymised. Contract, invoice and identification data is kept for the periods required by North Macedonian accounting, tax and transport rules, and for the limitation period of possible claims.

6. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability, subject to legal limits. You may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Agency of the Republic of North Macedonia. To exercise rights, email us using the address above.

7. Security and international transfers

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures. If data is processed outside North Macedonia or the EU/EEA, we use lawful transfer tools where required.

8. Children

Our services are intended for drivers who meet the minimum rental age. We do not knowingly collect data from children for marketing.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change. Continued use of the website after an update constitutes awareness of the revised text.
