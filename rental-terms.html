Rental conditions

Summary conditions · Very Special Rent a Car, Skopje · Effective 13 August 2026

These conditions summarise how Very Special Rent a Car hires vehicles. The signed rental agreement and any confirmation message prevail if there is a difference. The service and these documents are property of Very Special Rent a Car.

1. Driver and documents

The main driver must be at least 24, hold a valid Macedonian or foreign licence, and present passport or national ID plus a payment card in their name. Additional drivers must be declared, licensed and at least 24. We do not rent under 24. There is no young-driver surcharge from age 24.

2. Reservation

A website request is not a confirmed booking until we accept it. We may refuse a hire if documents, deposit or eligibility are not met.

3. Deposit, fuel and insurance

A refundable security deposit is authorised at pickup. Fuel is full-to-full. Compulsory third-party liability (AO) is on the vehicle as required by Macedonian law. Collision / kasko cover with an excess is contractual; extra waivers can be offered at confirmation. The hirer must inspect the vehicle with us before departure and report damage on return. Traffic fines are the driver’s responsibility.

4. Use of the vehicle

The vehicle may only be driven by authorised drivers, on public roads, in a careful manner. Off-road use, racing and sub-hire are forbidden. Cross-border travel is allowed only if agreed in writing.

It is forbidden to drive the vehicle under the influence of alcohol, narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances or any other substance that impairs the ability to drive. The same rule applies to every authorised driver. A breach of this rule is a breach of the hire: insurance may not pay, and the hirer is liable for damage, fines, seizure of the vehicle and all related costs.

5. Pickup and return

Locations: Skopje International Airport (near Skopje, Petrovec) and the centre of Skopje (the whole city-centre area — pickup is arranged with you in the centre). Late return may be charged. The vehicle must be returned with the same equipment, in a reasonably clean condition.

6. Cancellation

Unconfirmed requests may be cancelled free of charge. After confirmation, cancellation at least 48 hours before pickup is usually free; later cancellation or no-show may incur a fee stated at confirmation.

7. Law

Rental contracts are governed by the law of the Republic of North Macedonia, with courts in Skopje, without prejudice to mandatory consumer rights.

1. Shoferi dhe dokumentet

Shoferi kryesor duhet të jetë të paktën 24 vjeç, me patentë të vlefshme maqedonase ose të huaj, pasaportë ose letërnjoftim dhe kartë në emrin e tij. Shoferët shtesë duhen deklaruar dhe të jenë të paktën 24 vjeç. Nën 24 vjeç nuk japim me qira. Nga 24 vjeç nuk ka tarifë shtesë për shofer të ri.

Kërkesa në faqe nuk është rezervim i konfirmuar derisa ta pranojmë.

Autorizohet depozitë e kthyeshme. Karburant plotë-në-plotë. Përgjegjësia e detyrueshme ndaj palëve të treta (AO) është mbi mjetin sipas ligjit. Kasko me franshizë është kontraktuale; heqje shtesë mund të ofrohen në konfirmim. Automjeti kontrollohet së bashku para nisjes. Gjobat i paguan shoferi.

Drejtojnë vetëm shoferët e autorizuar, në rrugë publike, me kujdes. Ndalohen terreni, garat dhe nënqiraja. Kalimi i kufirit vetëm me pëlqim me shkrim.

Kjo është shkelje e kontratës: sigurimi mund të mos paguajë, ndërsa dëmi, gjobat, bllokimi i mjetit dhe të gjitha shpenzimet e lidhura bien mbi qiramarrësin.

5. Marrja dhe kthimi

Lokacionet: Aeroporti i Shkupit (pranë qytetit, Petrovec) dhe qendra e Shkupit (e gjithë zona qendrore — marrja organizohet me ju në qendër). Kthimi me vonesë mund të tarifohet.

6. Anulimi

Kërkesat e pakonfirmuara — pa pagesë. Pas konfirmimit, të paktën 48 orë para marrjes zakonisht pa pagesë; më vonë ose mungesa mund të tarifohen.

7. Ligji
